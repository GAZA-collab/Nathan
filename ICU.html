About ICU

ICU's story began in South Korea in 1998 as a pioneering institution in ICT research and engineering.

History of ICU

1998 - Founded in Korea

ICU was established in South Korea in 1998 and became known for research and engineering in information and communication technology.

2006 - International Reach

By 2006, approximately 20% of enrolled graduate students were international students.

2009 - Merger With KAIST

On March 1, 2009, ICU merged into KAIST as the Information Technology Convergence Campus.

ICU's legacy continued through dedicated academics and researchers.

2009+ - ICU Reborn

A consortium of professors and directors of research centres worked to continue ICU's legacy through international training and distance education.
